Business Outlook

Glow Squad (Pty) Ltd is a 100% women-owned South African company specializing in professional cleaning services and office supply solutions. We deliver high-quality, reliable, and cost-effective services to residential, commercial, and corporate clients.

Vision & Mission

Vision

To become a leading women-owned service provider in South Africa, known for excellence in cleaning and office support services.

Mission

  • Deliver professional, efficient, and reliable cleaning services.
  • Provide quality office equipment and supplies at competitive prices.
  • Maintain high standards of hygiene, safety, and customer satisfaction.
  • Empower women through business leadership and job creation.

Competitive Advantage

  • 100% women-owned enterprise

  • Affordability and competitive pricing
